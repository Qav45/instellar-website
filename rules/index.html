Instellar

Rules

These rules apply to all players at all times. Staff decide every punishment, and the penalties listed are templates, not fixed sentences.

⚖️

Punishments are decided by staff. The penalty shown on each rule is a template: a starting point, not a fixed sentence. Staff may give a lighter or harsher punishment depending on severity, intent, and your past record.

🛡️
Rule #1

New Player Protection

Don't kill players with under 15 min of playtime.

Exception: if they steal from you, or return to reclaim their own dropped loot.

⚔️
Rule #2

No Spawn Killing

Wait 20 seconds before re-engaging a player who just respawned.

Void if they attack you first.

🏷️
Rule #3

No Begging for Ranks

Do not ask for ranks, OP, or free items.

Repeated requests = kick or warning
💙
Rule #4

Respect Everyone

No slurs, hate speech, or harassment. Applies to chat and Simple Voice Chat.

Punished by severity
🚫
Rule #5

No Cheating

Hacks, X-ray, killaura, freecam, etc. Exploiting bugs is also prohibited.

30 days to permanent ban
🏗️
Rule #6

No Inappropriate Building

No 18+ builds or lag machines.

Lag machines = server nuking charge
💬
Rule #7

Keep Chat PG-13

No NSFW content, excessive swearing, or chat spam at any time.

🧭
Rule #8

No Advertising

Don't post other server IPs in public chat.

Up to 5-day ban
🪂
Rule #9

No Elytra or Trident in Combat

No flying, riptide boosts, elytra-macing, or elytra-spearing while in active combat.

Warning to 10-day ban
🛒
Rule #10

No Spawn Pushing

Don't force players into spawn via boats, minecarts, or physical pushing.

Up to 15-day ban
⛓️
Rule #11

No Loopholing

Rules apply by intent, not just wording. Abusing loopholes = rulebreaking.

⏰
Rule #12

No Combat Logging

Don't disconnect or use /home to escape combat.

Warning to 1-day ban
🎣
Rule #13

No TPA Trapping

Don't use /tpa or /tpahere to lure and trap other players.

Up to 7-day ban
🚫
Rule #14

No Crystal PvP

End Crystal use in combat is BANNED. No exceptions whatsoever.

Warning to permanent ban
🔨
Rule #15

No Spear Spamming

Do not spam the spear.

🪶
Rule #16

No Slow Falling

Using Slow Falling to escape combat or gain an unfair advantage is BANNED.

Warning to 10-day ban
🪽
Rule #17

No Elytraing Immediately After Combat

Do not use an Elytra to fly away immediately after combat ends. If you fight, you must leave the area normally before taking flight.

Warning to 10-day ban
🧪
Rule #18

No Stasis Chambers

Stasis chambers are BANNED. Do not build, use, or rely on them to escape danger, combat, traps, or punishment.

Warning to permanent ban
🏚️
Rule #19

No Griefing Bases

Do not grief, destroy, or vandalize other players' bases or builds.

Warning to permanent ban
