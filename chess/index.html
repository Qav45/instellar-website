Chess camera

Track moves on a physical board and ask Stockfish for Black’s best move. Keep the camera fixed above the board.

1. Aim and align

Place the whole board in view. Start the camera, then tap its four outer board corners in order: top left, top right, bottom right, bottom left. Keep hands out of view when reading a move.

Camera images stay in your browser memory. No frame is uploaded or saved. The chess engine runs in your browser too.

2. Read each move

Begin with the standard setup or enter a FEN below for a game already in progress. After each physical move, press Read move and confirm the suggestion.
